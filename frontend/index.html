WHY HIREFLOW AI

Everything HR Needs to Assess Candidates

One AI-powered pipeline that replaces manual resume screening, first-round tests and first-round interviews.

AI Resume Analysis

Every uploaded resume is analyzed for skills, experience and role fit before an invite ever goes out.

Role-Based Knowledge Test

A different, AI-generated question set for every candidate — never the same test twice.

Hands-On Task for Every Role

Real code for developers, a technical lab for DevOps and security, written, data and design tasks for other roles, and call simulations for sales and support. Scored automatically; HR reads one report, not raw answers.

AI Voice Interview

A spoken, adaptive interview delivered live over LiveKit: role questions, how the candidate would use AI in the job, salary expectations and a closing question — with follow-ups based on what the candidate actually says.
