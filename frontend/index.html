The questions come from the person, not a template.

When a candidate opens their link, HireFlow builds their profile from the resume HR uploaded. The tests and the interview are built from that resume and the job, so two candidates for the same role are not asked the same thing.

What we will not do.

Some of this is a limit we chose on purpose, and we say so.

Make the decision for you.

Scores and integrity signals are evidence. The report never recommends a hiring decision.

Scrape a candidate's profiles.

We do not read LinkedIn or Naukri behind a login. Candidates share their own resume and links, with their knowledge.

Hide how it works.

Candidates read the rules before they start. HR sees what was recorded and why a flag was raised.
